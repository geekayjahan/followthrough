## Pack, delivery, documents

- Micro lots come in 60 or 70 kg jute sacks with GrainPro liners; nano lots in smaller bags. Default pack 50 kg in the ops notes; smaller sacks at an extra bagging cost. Check which applies to the next shipment.
- Collect from the warehouse near Bristol (Devon in one email), or delivered with a last-mile haulage charge; direct delivery from the port is cheaper on pre-orders.
- Certificate of origin with every order; proof of delivery by email; both stored against the order.
- Lead time quoted: 4 to 7 weeks on one thread, 6 to 8 on another. Check the figure to use.
- Pre-order swap: a pre-ordered lot can be swapped for another from the same shipment if it does not suit, subject to availability.
- Hold: lots are held for a named date ("till Monday", "closing orders Friday 30 October"), never open-ended.

## Payment

- First order: 50% deposit on order, balance before dispatch (quoted to one roaster). Early-stage: upfront payment, with a payment terms contract if that helps. Credit terms (net 14 or 30) only after an order history.
- Harvest bookings: roaster pays a deposit (30% or 50%, decision open), GK pays the estate once deposits cover the order; title stays with Kāma until paid in full; deposit refunded only if Kāma cannot deliver.
- Never: a first-order discount, free equipment, exclusivity, a volume discount below floor, a promise of availability beyond the lots in stock or allocated.

## Samples

- Green samples 300 g, or 1 kg for lots to share with several roasters; roasted samples for cafés. Sample stock is deducted per send.
- Chase 5 days after a sample is sent; 7 days after an outreach email with no reply (ops notes). After a reply, the next move follows the thread.
- Closing dates drive urgency: every chase near a shipment names the closing date.

## Stall days

- Pitch fees seen: £16 (suitcase pitch), £20 to £30 (weekly market), £28 (farmers' market), £38 with hired canopy, £60 plus VAT (first pitch at one market). Invoiced after the market or paid in advance; QuickBooks or Xero invoices from organisers.
- Takings by SumUp card reader and cash, banked to the main account.
- Before a market: insurance certificate (£5m public and product liability), food business registration proof, signed application form, roast slot booked, bags labelled (labels from the roaster), card reader tested, table, parasol or gazebo with weights, a bin for tasters.
- Bags to take: about 50 to 55 for a good Sunday market; a slow Saturday sold 25.

## Outreach limits (deliverability and PECR)

- Cold sends capped at 20 to 30 a day, spread across the day; plain text, one link, personalised first line; pause a batch above 3% bounces.
- Limited companies and LLPs may be cold emailed with an opt-out line; sole traders and partnerships need consent, so phone or post instead (Companies House check per lead).
- Suppression list kept even after a contact is erased; dead leads deleted after a retention period (12, 24 or 36 months, decision open).
